Our Products

Three platforms, each built to remove a costly point of friction in its market.

Cross-Border Mobility Platform (Islamabad & AJK Corridor)

Premium Zero-Trust Mobility & Asset Security Marketplace

Eliminating the trust barrier in high-value vehicle rentals through NADRA-linked AI identity verification, automated digital escrow holds, and API-triggered GPS immobilization.

  • Shufti Pro AI KYC

    Biometric facial matching and CNIC/passport verification.

  • SafePay Digital Escrow

    Automated security deposit authorization holds.

  • TPL Trakker Integration

    Real-time geofencing and remote asset protection.

  • Offline-First Handover

    SQLite local caching for low-signal mountain routes.

B2B2C SaaS & Route Optimization

Intelligent Scheduling & Revenue Protection for Driving Instructors

Transforming how UK Approved Driving Instructors (ADIs) manage their diaries by replacing manual bookings with automated, postcode-aware route planning and hard-coded cancellation fee rules.

  • Postcode Travel Matrix

    Calculates exact travel time between lesson locations to optimize fuel and schedule gaps.

  • DVSA Test-Date Auto-Matching

    Dynamically aligns pupil schedules with local driving test availability.

  • Upfront Escrow & Payment

    Enforces cancellation rules and secures instructor earnings automatically via Stripe.

B2B Micro-SaaS / EPOS Bridge

Cascade Inventory Sync & Middleware for Cafes & Dessert Parlours

A cloud-based API bridge connecting EPOS systems (like Square) directly to Uber Eats, Just Eat, and Deliveroo to prevent stockouts and eliminate cancelled order penalties.

  • Cascade Ingredient-Level Syncing

    Automatically pauses multi-item menu options the moment a single underlying ingredient runs out on the till.

  • Complex Modifier Mapping

    Eliminates dropped order customization data between delivery apps and kitchen printers.

  • Peak-Rush Auto-Throttling

    Dynamically extends preparation times during high-volume hours to protect kitchen operations.
